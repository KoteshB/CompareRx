High-risk pairs

  • 8 Sompraz D + 15 Storvas CV Esomeprazole weakens the clopidogrel in Storvas CV.
  • 4 Planep + 2 Sartel H Eplerenone and telmisartan both raise potassium.
  • 5 Rexipra Forte + 8 Sompraz D, 7 Formonide, 11 Starizo QT prolongation and serotonin syndrome; esomeprazole also raises escitalopram levels.
  • 13 Jalra M + 6 Lactihep, 2 Sartel H, 4 Planep Dehydration and kidney strain let metformin build up (lactic acidosis).
  • 10 Cetil CV + 11 Starizo Two antibiotics at once; confirm both are needed.

Questions to bring to the doctor

  1. Can esomeprazole be swapped for a PPI that doesn't interfere with clopidogrel?
  2. Can potassium, sodium, magnesium, kidney function and an ECG be checked at the 5-day review?
  3. Are all four blood-pressure drugs and both antibiotics needed?
  4. Is the escitalopram plus tedizolid combination acceptable?

No ingredient appears twice on the list, so there is no direct double dose as written. Avoid extra clonazepam, escitalopram, diuretics or potassium products on top of these.
